Quantum-Enhanced MCMC for Financial Contagion

A tiered interbank network (EGJ cross-holdings) as an Ising model. Watch a quantum proposal (QeMCMC, Trotterised and sampled in your browser) race classical Metropolis to learn the bailout-susceptibility landscape, then spend a rescue budget where it actually closes the cascade.
